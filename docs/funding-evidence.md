# Finnish UAS funding evidence

Checked 28 November 2026. The dashboard uses 2025 results, the published 2025 Arcada award at signing and the 2026 national budget. The 2025 observation describes educational output. A three-year bachelor’s window of 2023–2025 is the working baseline for 2027 funding. The government’s 2027 budget proposal continues to cite decree 171/2024. Keep activity year, funding year, source release and decision vintage distinct. ## Allocation and timing

The model in force from 1 January 2025 is 75% education, 20% research, development and innovation, and 5% strategy. Weights in the table below apply to the whole model. For an indicator, the denominator is the sum of the eligible, weighted results of all institutions in the ministry allocation for the same vintage. Each institution’s share is its result divided by that denominator. Use the three latest available calendar years averaged separately for each indicator. First-time entrants use only the latest available year. The ministry prepares figures in June ahead of the funding year and issues the decision around year-end. Where final observations are available with the usual lag, a 2026 funding calculation would use 2022–2024 and a 2027 calculation would use 2023–2025. The latest entry year can differ between the June estimate and the final decision. Employment has a further register lag. Confirm each exact data window and any later corrections against the frozen calculation before quoting an allocation or scenario. [Government decree 1129/2014, §§12–14](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2014/1129) · [Decree 171/2024, §§1–4 and annex](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2024/171) · [OKM indicator definitions](https://wiki.eduuni.fi/download/attachments/39984987/Ammattikorkeakoulujen%20perusrahoituksen%20laskennassa%20k%C3%A4ytett%C3%A4v%C3%A4t%20indikaattorit.pdf) · [Ministry’s 2026 decision timetable](https://pk.xamk.fi/D10_Julkaisu/kokous/2026649-10-92396.PDF)

In funding years 2025 and 2026, the non-strategy award could fall by at most 3% relative to the previous year after rescaling to the current funding pool. A resulting shortfall was distributed proportionately. Section 4 names those two years. The published Xamk decision and calculation show why to retain revision history: the ministry’s attachment references 2022–2023 R&D corrections. [Decree 171/2024, §4](https://www.finlex.fi/sv/lagstiftning/2024/171) · [Ministry decision and calculation, 19 December 2025](https://pk.xamk.fi/D10_Julkaisu/kokous/2026649-7-92713.PDF)

Arcada’s agreement of 19 December 2024 records **€16.418m** in 2025 state funding at signing, including €14.141m calculated from indicators, €0.650m strategy, €1.468m VAT compensation and €0.159m discretionary EU research co-financing. It assigns 4Q network funding to Kajaani UAS. The 2026 budget appropriated €939.168m for UAS base funding within the €1,041.011m state-funding budget. Arcada’s final 2026 line-by-line calculation and a frozen sector input file remain unverified. A 56% model weight does not tell us Arcada’s bachelor allocation. [Agreement, §§4–5](https://okm.fi/documents/1410845/3991460/Yrkesh%C3%B6gskolan%2BArcada%2Bavtal%2B2025-2028.pdf/d8e3f5a8-d62c-9e9a-b2b3-591a98dab230?t=1738563519564&version=1.2) · [2026 Finnish state budget, 29.40.55](https://budjetti.vm.fi/sisalto.jsp?lang=fi&maindoc=%2F2026%2Faky%2Faky.xml&opennode=0%3A1%3A11%3A269%3A719%3A793%3A803%3A&year=0)

To price a future scenario, reproduce a published line from its component pool `P`, Arcada baseline `A` and sector denominator `S`. If the only changes are Arcada’s eligible weighted inputs after applying caps, change `d` also changes the denominator. The unadjusted difference is `P × ((A + d)/(S + d) − A/S)`. Use the actual year-specific transition adjustments, corrections and rounding when reconciling a final amount. Preserve the distinction between a one-year change diluted over a three-year window and a persistent change. Keep published allocation and scenario in separate fields, with named assumptions. No euro value is assigned to the 95-award gap.

## Funding components and usable evidence

Whole-model weights follow the published decree annex and the ministry’s current chart. The denominator for each row is the same eligible quantity across funded UASs. Caps and multipliers are listed where applicable. A three-year window always refers to latest available observations for that indicator. The public operational benchmark on the dashboard uses a different denominator, the awards counted in all three time bands for the scoped field. [Ministry decision and annexes](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/paatokset/paatos?decisionId=1148) · [Ministry model chart](https://okm.fi/documents/1410845/4392480/AMK_rahoitusmalli%20vuodesta%202025%20alkaen.pdf/192b7ef5-af61-d760-3f5b-547b3eed63a4?t=1754898675830)

The current Finnish consolidated XML reverses the two education weights for vocational teacher education and cooperation credits. The original Finnish and Swedish annexes, consolidated Swedish text, ministry chart and HAMK’s 2025 indicator report agree on 2% for teacher education and 1% for cooperation credits. The dashboard follows those sources. Get written ministry confirmation if using either line in a financial reproduction. [Original Finnish annex](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2024/171) · [Consolidated Finnish XML](https://opendata.finlex.fi/finlex/avoindata/v1/akn/fi/act/statute-consolidated/2024/171/fin@latest) · [Swedish annex](https://www.finlex.fi/api/media/statute/5600/mainPdf/main.pdf?timestamp=2024-04-11T00%3A00%3A00.000Z#page=5) · [HAMK 2025 annual report](https://www.hamk.fi/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/toimintakertomus-ja-tilinpaatos-2025.pdf)

## Health and welfare case

Annual report Table 2 gives 226, 217 and 205 bachelor’s awards in 2023–2025. The 2025 award count was 95 below the agreement’s 300 annual average target, and 12 lower than 2024. The agreement’s cap group also includes humanities and medicine. The operational comparison covers the health and welfare field recorded in Vipunen. Twelve commissioned nursing awards (four nurse and eight public health nurse) account for the difference between 205 and 217 under all funding sources in 2025. [Arcada annual report, Table 2](https://www.arcada.fi/sites/default/files/2026-04/Arcada%20University%20of%20Applied%20Sciences%20Annual%20Report%202025%20in%20Swedish.pdf) · [Agreement, §3.2](https://okm.fi/ammattikorkeakoulut-sopimukset)

The 2025 completion extract, dated 23 March 2026 and updated 27 November 2026, contained 3,089 public aggregate rows before the commissioning exclusion. It gave 157 on time, 28 up to 12 months late and 20 later at Arcada. Across the 21 institutions with qualifying field awards, the corresponding counts were 8,342, 1,497 and 696. Both final Vipunen resources reconcile for the same institution-by-institution denominator, and Arcada matches the annual report. The difference is 2.6 percentage points after rounding. Arcada’s largest national qualification by award volume is nursing (65). The seven national qualification codes are visible on the page. [Completion-time source](https://api.vipunen.fi/api/resources/tavoiteajassa_tutkinnon_suorittaneet) · [Awards source](https://api.vipunen.fi/api/resources/opiskelijat_ja_tutkinnot)

The 2024–2025 drop was concentrated in nursing (code `671101`, 92 to 65) and social services (`671201`, 46 to 40). Higher physiotherapy awards (`671112`, 18 to 37) offset part of the decline. The nursing series was 63 in 2023, so a single year-to-year drop warrants a cohort check. The agreement calls for nursing intake to stay at least at the 2022 level. [Vipunen awards source](https://api.vipunen.fi/api/resources/opiskelijat_ja_tutkinnot) · [Agreement, §3.2](https://okm.fi/ammattikorkeakoulut-sopimukset)

The matched nursing qualification comparison was 51 of 65 on time at Arcada (78.5%) and 2,935 of 3,778 nationally (77.7%). Public non-commissioned starts for this qualification were 77 in 2022, 72 in 2023, 177 in 2024 and 304 in 2025. These are new starters across routes. They do not measure first-time entrants for the funding formula or the completion of those same cohorts. Arcada lists recognition of prior learning and a shorter expected duration on its TopUp route. Review intake routes and placement capacity before diagnosing delays. [Vipunen completion times](https://api.vipunen.fi/api/resources/tavoiteajassa_tutkinnon_suorittaneet) · [Vipunen starters](https://api.vipunen.fi/api/resources/opiskelijat_ja_tutkinnot) · [Arcada Nursing TopUp](https://www.arcada.fi/en/study-arcada/bachelors-degree-programmes/nursing-topup)

The 48 late awards describe graduates who completed in 2025. To assess whether cohort size, retention, clinical placements, thesis completion or other factors drove the shortfall, pair recent entry cohorts with local progression and completion milestones. Use the signed programme mapping and institutional analysis environment. Ask the nursing programme director to name one suspected bottleneck and the next cohort review date.
